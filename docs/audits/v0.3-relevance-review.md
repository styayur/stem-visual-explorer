# v0.3 relevance-annotation review

The first precision run failed three per-query gates. We inspected the actual titles and corrected the direct-relevance labels before rerunning **both** the untouched baseline and the new engine. This is an annotation correction, not an algorithm gain. The original rules remain in `artifacts/precision-hints-initial.json`; original detailed results remain in `artifacts/retrieval-benchmark-initial-hints.json.gz`. All 36 important queries and every hard false-positive probe remain in the final benchmark.

| Query | Initial rule error | Reviewed direct relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Maxwell equations | `maxwell` incorrectly labeled **Maxwell Velocity** as relevant. That simulation concerns speed distributions, not electromagnetic equations. | Require Maxwell + equation (or its Chinese label); explicitly exclude Maxwell velocity/speed/distribution. The shipped index has no direct Maxwell-equation witness. |
| Taylor series | `taylor` counted finite Taylor-polynomial error/tolerance pages as direct series resources. Finite polynomials are a distinct ontology concept. | Direct series/expansion titles are positives; polynomial-only titles are not direct equivalents. Taylor polynomial remains an explicit related concept and is available via exploration. |
| Conservation of energy | `energy` counted every energy title as conservation, including **Work Energy Theorem Power** and **Energy Forms and Changes**. | Require conservation or the specifically witnessed energy-balance simulations Energy Skate Park/Energy Visualiser. Unjudged generic energy titles do not establish direct relevance. |

This narrower direct-match judgment aligns with the requested Direct/Equivalent/Exploratory separation. It does **not** establish that related learning material is unhelpful. Under the original broad hints, strict direct search reduced Taylor-polynomial recall and generic-energy recall; that tradeoff is preserved in the archived initial report. Users can explicitly explore related concepts.

No minimum was reduced: 172 witnessed concepts retain mandatory resources, with several key concepts tightened from one to two results. Unknown coverage cases remain visible in the zero-result metric. Precision remains a title-hint proxy, not a substitute for a future full-document human relevance set.
